Delivery, returns and refills

When will my order arrive?

We post Monday to Friday. UK orders arrive in 1–3 working days; international orders in 4–10.

Can I return an opened product?

Yes, within 30 days. Tell us what didn't work and we'll refund you or suggest something better suited.

How do refills work?

Send back five empty amber bottles or jars with the prepaid label in your parcel and we'll send a free refill of your choice.

Are your products natural?

They're botanical, not purist. We combine plants with lab-made actives like retinal and vitamin C when they work better than any plant alternative.
