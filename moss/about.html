Our apothecary

A converted dairy in the Peak District, a still, a lot of amber glass and four people.

One plant, one active, one percentage

Most labels list thirty ingredients and tell you nothing about how much of each. We build every formula around a single botanical and a single proven active, and we print the percentage of that active on the front.

Plants come from growers we visit. Actives come from suppliers who share their stability data. Everything is mixed in batches of four hundred, so nothing sits in a warehouse for a year.

What we hold ourselves to

Traceable plants
Every botanical is named with its origin and harvest month on our website.
Printed percentages
The key active and its amount are on the front label, not hidden in the list.
Amber glass and refills
Light-proof glass bottles and a free refill for every five empties returned.
Gentle by default
No added fragrance in leave-on formulas. Every batch is patch-tested.
