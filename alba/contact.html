Questions we hear often

How long does shipping take?

Orders leave the atelier within two working days. Delivery takes 3–5 working days in Europe and 5–8 elsewhere.

Can I return a product?

Yes, within 30 days, even if it's been opened. Write to us and we'll send a prepaid label.

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

All leave-on formulas are fragrance-free and tested on sensitive skin. Introduce one new product at a time.

Do you test on animals?

Never. Our formulas and raw materials are not tested on animals anywhere in the world.
