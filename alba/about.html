The atelier

A small laboratory in Grasse, a short list of actives, and a lot of patience.

Why porcelain

Alba began with a single blue-and-white plate and a question: what would skincare look like if it were made with the same discipline? One material, one colour, fired until it is flawless.

We work in batches of two thousand units so every bottle reaches you within months of filling. Each formula is tested by an independent dermatology lab on at least thirty volunteers before it carries our name.

Our standards

Actives at proven strengths

We list the percentage of every key active on the pack, and we only use levels supported by published studies.

Gentle by design

No added fragrance in leave-on products, no drying alcohols, no essential oils. Suitable for sensitive skin.

Glass and refills

Glass bottles, aluminium caps and refill pouches for our two best-loved formulas.

Letters from the atelier

New formulas, refills and the occasional ritual. Once a month, never more.
